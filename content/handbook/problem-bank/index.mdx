The bank holds the 173 curriculum problems (114 Core, 39 Stretch, 20 Optional), in the order you meet them. Assessment and mock problems are listed separately in section 9 so they stay unseen. No problem requires LeetCode Premium. Every number, title, URL and difficulty in this bank and in sections 1 and 9 was checked against the doocs/leetcode mirror of LeetCode (current to Weekly Contest 525), and nine were also checked on leetcode.com. LeetCode occasionally re-rates or rewrites problems (190 changed recently), so if a live page differs, trust the live page.

**Columns.** *When* = the module and lesson that teach the problem. *Tier* = C/S/O as in the lessons. *Role* = position in the pattern ladder: **Intro** (teaches the pattern), **Core** (the canonical problem), **Transfer** (same idea in a new setting), **Challenge** (harder, optional value). Every problem is a first-time attempt in its lesson; it then becomes a revision problem in the tracker (section 8). Read the hint only after 10 minutes of independent thinking.

Module 12 rows are for after your attempt: do not read their Pattern or Hint columns beforehand.


**Bank totals check.** Phase 1: 45 problems; Phase 2: 51; Phase 3: 43; Phase 4: 34. Total 173 = 114 Core + 39 Stretch + 20 Optional. In this edition 150, 973 and 455 moved from Core to Stretch so that no lesson needs more than about 50 minutes of new-problem practice. Module 12's lesson 4 Hard is chosen from problems already listed here (42, 76, 84, 124, 239, 295, 297); 239 ([Sliding Window Maximum](https://leetcode.com/problems/sliding-window-maximum/), Hard, monotonic deque) and 297 ([Serialize and Deserialize Binary Tree](https://leetcode.com/problems/serialize-and-deserialize-binary-tree/), Hard, preorder with null markers) appear only as those Hard options.
