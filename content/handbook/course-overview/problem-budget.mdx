| Bucket | Count | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core curriculum problems | 114 | Required; about 2 per lesson |
| Stretch problems | 39 | Optional, outside the 144 hours; aim for at least 15–25 |
| Optional Hard/advanced | 20 | Pick 4–8 that interest you |
| Assessment problems (unseen) | 13 | Simulation conditions |
| Mock interview problems (unseen) | 6 | One per mock; the other 12 pool problems become extra practice |
| **Realistic unique total** | **\~150–160** | Minimum: 133 (Core + assessments + mocks) |
| Planned re-solves | 90 | 56 lesson warm-ups + 28 at checkpoints (including each assessment's cold re-solve) + 6 in Modules 11–12 |

Why not 300? Solving 150 problems with spaced re-solves and honest review produces better transfer to unseen problems than rushing through 300 once. Interviews test derivation, not recall.
