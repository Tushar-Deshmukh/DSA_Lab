Score A out of 12 and B out of 5 (one point per task solved within its time box with correct complexity stated; B5 earns its point only if both parts are correct). Apply the three rules below in order and stop at the first one that matches, so every one of the 78 possible score pairs leads to exactly one profile.

| Order | Rule (first match wins) | Profile | What changes in Modules 1–3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A ≤ 5 **or** B ≤ 1 | **Foundations** | Before Module 1, spend about 2 h on section 6 parts 6.1–6.3 and re-do B1–B3; in Modules 1–3 do Core only and leave Stretch for afterwards |
| 2 | A ≥ 10 **and** B ≥ 4 | **Accelerated** | Skim Module 1 theory except the JS cost model; treat every Stretch problem in Modules 1–3 as Core, using the time saved on theory; add the optional Hard of each module if time remains |
| 3 | Anything else (A 6–12 with B 2–5 that rule 2 did not match) | **Standard** | Follow the plan exactly as written |

Targeted fixes, whatever your profile:

- **Missed Q1–Q4 or Q8–Q9:** read the Module 1 theory, "Complexity notation" and "Using constraints" parts twice and do the six snippets in its Example 2 before lesson 1.
- **Missed Q5–Q7 or Q11:** read section 6.1–6.4 (JS cost model, Map/Set, sorting, bitwise) before lesson 1.
- **Failed B4:** linked lists will need extra care; in Module 4, do all the Stretch problems.
- **Failed B5 binary search:** in Module 3, do lesson 1 twice and skip lesson 4's Stretch.
