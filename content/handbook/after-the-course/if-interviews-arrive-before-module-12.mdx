Prioritise by frequency, in this order, using the Core problems only: Modules 1–2 (arrays, hashing, windows), Module 6 (trees), Module 8 (graphs), Module 3 (binary search), Module 4 (lists and stacks), Module 10 (DP I), Module 7 (heaps and intervals), Module 5 (backtracking). Do at least two mocks before the first interview, even if that costs a topic.
