| Session | Duration | Activity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 60 min | One unseen Medium (timed, 35 min) + one tracker re-solve |
| 2 | 60 min | One unseen Medium from your weakest pattern + one re-solve |
| 3 | 2 h | Alternate: a mock interview (45 + 15 min) or a timed set of 2 Mediums + 1 Hard attempt; then scorecard update |
| 4 | 60–90 min (optional) | LeetCode weekly contest (check its current schedule on LeetCode) or one deferred topic from 12.2 |

Keep the tracker and spaced repetition running: about 8–10 new problems and 8 re-solves a week. Every four weeks, take one Assessment-style set using problems you have not seen, and compare your scores with Module 12.
