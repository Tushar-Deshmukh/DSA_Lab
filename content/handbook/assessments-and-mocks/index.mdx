Six timed assessments (Modules 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12) measure what you can do alone; six mock interviews (Modules 5, 7, 9, 11 and twice in Module 12) measure what you can do while explaining. All new problems here are kept out of the bank so they stay unseen until you start the session.
