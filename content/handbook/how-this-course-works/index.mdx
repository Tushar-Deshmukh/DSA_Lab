You will study **about 144 hours across 12 modules**, plus about 3.5 hours for the placement check and the final self-assessment. You will attempt **about 150 unique LeetCode problems** (133 are planned; the rest are optional Stretch problems, outside the 144 hours) and complete **90 planned re-solves**, with six assessments and six mock interviews. The plan optimises for one outcome: solving an unseen medium problem in 35–40 minutes while explaining your reasoning out loud.
