- **Missed by one or two measures:** continue with the next module; spend your next two extra sessions on the weakest measure (e.g. low re-solve rate → only re-solves in those sessions).
- **Missed by three or more measures at Module 4 or 8:** do one *consolidation round* before continuing. Re-do the Core problems of the two weakest modules as cold re-solves, then a re-take of the most recent assessment with the unused problems from the matching mock pool.
- **Missed at Module 12:** this is common and fine. Use section 12's plan for 4–6 more weeks before scheduling demanding interview loops, and focus on the measures you missed.

Why these targets: they are set for about 2 hours per lesson. A 60–70% unseen-Medium solve rate with clear communication is a realistic outcome of this course; consistent Hard performance is not, and the plan does not pretend otherwise.
