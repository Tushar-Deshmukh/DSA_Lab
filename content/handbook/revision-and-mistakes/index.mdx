Every attempted problem enters the tracker straight away. The course has 90 planned re-solve slots for about 150 problems, so not every problem can get every interval: the slots go to the problems that need them most (rules below). The target intervals are roughly +1, +3, +7 and +14 days, and a problem leaves active revision after three clean re-solves at least a week apart.
