| Interval | Where it happens | Which problems |
| --- | --- | --- |
| +1 day | The next lesson's 10-minute warm-up (named in the lesson) | The hardest Core problem of the previous lesson |
| +3 days | A later warm-up or the checkpoint review | Problems from lessons 1–3 reviewed at the checkpoint |
| +7 days | The next module's checkpoint review | 1–2 problems from the previous module |
| +14 days | A checkpoint review two modules later | 1 problem per review |
| +30 days | Module 12 repair block and the after-course plan | Anything solved independently three times |

**Adaptive rules.**

- Re-solved cleanly (no hints, within the time box) → double the next interval (3 → 7 → 14 → 30).
- Re-solved with a hint or over time → keep the same interval.
- Failed → next revision in 2 days, and change your study action by mistake type (8.3).
- **Mastered** = derived, implemented, explained and analysed without help on three occasions at least 7 days apart. Mastered problems leave active revision; sample one per module in Modules 10–12.
- When more problems are ready than slots available, fill the slots in this order: failed or "needed solution" problems, then Core problems solved with a hint, then Core problems not yet re-solved, then the longest-waiting of the rest. A problem that never reaches a slot gets one cold re-solve in the Module 12 repair block or in the after-course plan (section 12).
