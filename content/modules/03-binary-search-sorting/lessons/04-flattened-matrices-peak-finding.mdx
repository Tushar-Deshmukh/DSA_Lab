<Unit id="peaks-without-sorted-data" kind="theory" title="Peaks without sorted data">

Find Peak Element (162) asks for any index whose value is greater than both neighbours, with `nums[−1] = nums[n] = −∞`. The array is not sorted, yet O(log n) is possible. Look at `mid` and its right neighbour. If the right neighbour is larger, you are walking uphill: keep going right and either the values keep rising until the end (the last element is then a peak, because `nums[n] = −∞`) or they fall at some point (the top before the fall is a peak). Either way a peak exists right of `mid`. Otherwise `mid` is a peak or the slope goes up to the left, and by the mirror argument a peak exists in `[lo, mid]`.
