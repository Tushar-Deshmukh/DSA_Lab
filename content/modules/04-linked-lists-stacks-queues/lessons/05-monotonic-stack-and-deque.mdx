<Unit id="distances-and-widths" kind="theory" title="Distances and widths">

Storing indices is what turns "the next greater value" into "how many days until it" (739): when index j is popped at i, the wait is `i − j`.

<Viz id="daily-temps" />

Largest Rectangle in Histogram (84, Stretch) uses both neighbours at once. When bar `top` is popped by a lower bar `i`, the bar below it on the stack is its previous smaller bar, and `i` is its next smaller bar, so the widest rectangle of height `h[top]` spans between them.
