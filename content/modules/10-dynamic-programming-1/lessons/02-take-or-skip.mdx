House Robber II (213) puts the houses in a circle, so the first and last are neighbours. At most one of them is robbed, so run 198 twice: once without the last house and once without the first, and take the larger. Delete and Earn (740, Optional) needs a bigger transformation: taking a value earns every copy of it but deletes the values one above and below, which is exactly "no two adjacent" over the sorted values.
