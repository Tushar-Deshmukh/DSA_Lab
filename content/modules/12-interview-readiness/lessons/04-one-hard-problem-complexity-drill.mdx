<Unit id="the-hard-problem" kind="theory" title="The Hard problem">

Choose one you have not solved: [42 Trapping Rain Water](https://leetcode.com/problems/trapping-rain-water/), [76 Minimum Window Substring](https://leetcode.com/problems/minimum-window-substring/), [84 Largest Rectangle in Histogram](https://leetcode.com/problems/largest-rectangle-in-histogram/), [124 Binary Tree Maximum Path Sum](https://leetcode.com/problems/binary-tree-maximum-path-sum/), [239 Sliding Window Maximum](https://leetcode.com/problems/sliding-window-maximum/), [295 Find Median from Data Stream](https://leetcode.com/problems/find-median-from-data-stream/), [297 Serialize and Deserialize Binary Tree](https://leetcode.com/problems/serialize-and-deserialize-binary-tree/). Give it 40 minutes of your own work, then use the hint ladder on the Revision rules page one rung at a time, and record in the journal which rung you needed. Then close everything and explain it from scratch.

<Callout kind="key" title="What makes a Hard hard">

Most Hards are a pattern you know plus one twist: a window that must also track counts (76), a stack that also measures widths (84), a tree return value that differs from the answer you track (124). Ask "which pattern almost works, and what does it fail to keep track of?"
