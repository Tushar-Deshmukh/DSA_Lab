<Include id="theory/interview-readiness/practice-structure" />

Open the Practice page and filter by status: the patterns with the most "Attempted" and "Needs revision" entries, and the fewest "Solved independently", are your weak ones. Use the Progress page's mistake tally too: a cluster of P (did not recognise the pattern) points at recognition, while A (could not derive the algorithm) points at the pattern's theory example. For each weak pattern, re-solve two tracker problems cold, then re-read that pattern's "Recognise" and "Clues" lines.
