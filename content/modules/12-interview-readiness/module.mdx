<Unit id="how-this-module-fits-together" kind="overview" title="How this module fits together">

There is no new theory in this module. The work is **retrieval under pressure**: picking the right tool from forty-odd patterns when nobody tells you the topic, inside a 45-minute structure, while explaining yourself. Every lesson pairs a short method lesson with timed practice.

| Lesson | Focus | Evidence you produce |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Rubric, 45-minute structure, recognition checklist | Mixed set A: pattern named within 8 minutes, 90-second explanations |
| 2 | Weak-pattern repair; abandoning, unsticking, hints | Two weak patterns re-solved cold; mixed set B |
| 3 | **Mock 5**; trade-offs and assumptions | Mock scored; one more timed problem |
| 4 | One Hard problem with the hint ladder; complexity drill | Hard solved and re-explained; 10 complexities stated |
| 5 | **Mock 6**; verbal drill | Mock scored; five 90-second explanations |
| Checkpoint | **Final Assessment 6** | Final scorecard |
| Final | Final self-assessment and follow-on plan | Readiness checklist completed honestly |

<Callout kind="warn" title="Unlabeled on purpose">

This module's problems hide their pattern until you have attempted them. Do not look them up in the problem bank first: the skill you are training is recognising the pattern yourself. If you are short on time, skip lesson 4's Hard problem first, then mixed set B. Never skip the mocks or the final assessment: they produce the evidence for your final self-assessment.

</Callout>
