summary:
  - k largest → min-heap of size k (its root is the weakest candidate). k smallest → max-heap.
  - O(n log k) time, O(k) space; better than sorting when k ≪ n or the data streams.
  - 'Quickselect: O(n) average for one k-th element; bucket: O(n) when keys are bounded counts.'
  - 'K-way merge: heap of the k current heads; pop the minimum, push its successor. O(N log k).'
