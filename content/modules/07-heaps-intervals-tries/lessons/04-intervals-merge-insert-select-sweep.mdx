<Unit id="keeping-the-most-intervals-sort-by-end" kind="theory" title="Keeping the most intervals: sort by end">

Non-overlapping Intervals (435) asks for the minimum number of removals, which is the same as keeping as many compatible intervals as possible. Sort by **end** and keep every interval that starts at or after the end of the last one kept. Why end and not start: the interval that ends first leaves the most room for everything after it, so replacing any optimal solution's first interval with it never makes things worse (the activity-selection exchange argument, proven in Module 9).
