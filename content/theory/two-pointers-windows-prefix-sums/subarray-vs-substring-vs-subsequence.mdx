**Subarray vs substring vs subsequence.** A *subarray* (arrays) and a *substring* (strings) are contiguous; there are O(n²) of them. A *subsequence* keeps relative order but may skip elements; there are O(2ⁿ). Contiguous problems invite windows and prefix sums. Subsequence problems usually need DP (Modules 10–11) or greedy.
