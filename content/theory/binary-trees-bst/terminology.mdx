**Terminology.** Root (no parent), leaf (no children), depth of a node (edges from the root), height of a node (edges on the longest path down to a leaf; LeetCode often counts nodes instead, so read the definition), subtree, ancestor/descendant. A *binary tree* has at most two children per node. A *full* tree: every node has 0 or 2 children. A *complete* tree: all levels full except possibly the last, filled left to right (heaps, Module 7). A *balanced* tree: heights of left and right subtrees differ by at most 1 at every node, so height is O(log n). A *skewed* tree degenerates into a linked list, height O(n): recursion depth then equals n.
