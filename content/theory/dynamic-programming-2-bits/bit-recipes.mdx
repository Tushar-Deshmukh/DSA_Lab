**Bit recipes (i = bit position, 0 = least significant).** Check: `(x >> i) & 1`. Set: `x | (1 << i)`. Clear: `x & ~(1 << i)`. Toggle: `x ^ (1 << i)`. Remove the lowest set bit: `x & (x - 1)`. Isolate the lowest set bit: `x & -x`. Power of two: `x > 0 && (x & (x - 1)) === 0`.
